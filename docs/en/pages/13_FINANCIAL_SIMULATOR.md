# 🔮 Documentation Page: Financial Simulator & Projections

The **Financial Simulator** (`static/js/views/simulator.js`) is OmniBank Local's mid-to-long-term wealth forecasting and scenario modeling engine. It models the long-term impact of life decisions (real estate purchases, career transitions, new loans, sabbaticals, investment opportunities) across a 1 to 30-year horizon.

---

## 🎯 1. Vision & Local-First Philosophy

The simulator computes all cash-flow paths locally within your browser and the FastAPI backend:
- **Zero Data Leakage**: Your future financial projections, private dreams, and life plans are never shared with remote servers or online banking aggregators.
- **Data-Driven Grounding**: Unlike generic tools that rely on abstract national averages, OmniBank Local seeds projections using your actual historical spending, real income, and active recurring subscriptions.

---

## 🧭 2. Multi-Scenario Modeling & Comparison

The simulator supports creating and comparing isolated scenarios side-by-side:

### A. Baseline Scenario
- Automatically seeded using your current steady-state transactions (salaries, rent/mortgage, subscriptions, variable monthly averages).
- Acts as the benchmark control against which all proposed life changes are evaluated.

### B. Custom Alternative Scenarios
- Common archetypes: *"Primary Residence Acquisition"*, *"Career Change / Startup"*, *"Rental Property Investment"*, *"New Child Arrival"*, *"Early Retirement"*.
- 1-click scenario cloning enables exploring branching hypotheses without having to re-enter base data.
- Instant toggle switches to enable or disable specific events to test elasticity.

---

## 📅 3. Scheduled Timeline Events

The core building blocks of any simulation are time-stamped events anchored along a timeline:

| Event Type | Description | Practical Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **💥 One-Off Event** | A single lump-sum expense or income occurring on a specific date. | Vehicle acquisition, home renovation, down payment, inheritance, asset sale. |
| **🔄 Recurring Cash-Flow Change** | Ongoing structural shift in income or charges starting on a given date (with optional expiration). | €250/mo wage hike, 20-year mortgage repayment schedule, child support end. |

Each timeline event can be mapped to a specific budget category and assigned to an individual bank account (Checking, Savings, Money Market).

---

## 📈 4. Macro-Economic & Behavioral Modulators

For realistic long-term modeling, the simulator includes three configurable modulators:

1. **Annual Inflation Rate (%)**: Compounded annual escalation applied to daily living and operational expenses over the timeline.
2. **Historical Real-World Seasonality**: Rather than spreading annual costs flatly across 12 months, the engine reproduces observed historical cycles (summer holiday peaks, autumn school/tax surges, December holiday gifts).
3. **Savings Yield / Growth Rate (%)**: Compounded growth generated by balances held across interest-bearing savings and investment accounts.

---

## 📊 5. Visualizations & Decision Metrics

### Interactive Comparative Chart (Chart.js)
- Displays the **Baseline Scenario** (dashed curve) superimposed against the **Active Scenario** (solid vibrant line).
- Interactive tooltips display precise monthly balances and milestone events at each point along the curve.

### Core Decision Metric Cards
- **Projected Final Balance**: Estimated cumulative cash and liquid net worth at the end of the simulation horizon.
- **Net Differential**: Total financial advantage or deficit of the alternative scenario compared to the baseline.
- **Cash Flow Trough (Minimum Liquidity)**: The lowest recorded balance point across the timeline (essential for spotting overdraft risks or cash pinches).
- **Break-Even / Depletion Date**: The milestone where an investment turns net positive, or the date liquid reserves run dry if running a persistent deficit.

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

> [!NOTE]
